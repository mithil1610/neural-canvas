Axiom 1.3.0

• Smarter vault chat: Axiom now searches everything you've saved, not just your latest items, so answers about older documents are more accurate.
• Reminders at the right time: event times are read in your local time zone, including across daylight-saving changes.

• Fair daily limits: free-plan limits now reset every day.
• Faster subscription updates: your plan updates as soon as a purchase or restore completes.
• Complete account deletion: Delete Account & Purge Vault removes your files, notes, chats and account.
• Security and reliability improvements.
